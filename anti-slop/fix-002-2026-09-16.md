## One-line reasons (R-31)

- **Ink `#141414` on paper `#f6f1e4`:** the base field of neobrutalism; paper makes the white card read as a pinned-on document.
- **3px ink borders + hard offset shadows (zero blur):** one depth language ("sticker on paper"); used identically on card, inputs, button, error banner. No soft shadow anywhere (R-12).
- **Accent `#FFD23F`:** the single loud accent; black text on it = 12.8:1 (R-25).
- **Letterhead strip (10px yellow + 3px ink):** the repeated identity motif of the screen (R-20).
- **Archivo Black for the title only:** carries the loud display voice of the style; Space Grotesk handles all form text for readability (R-06).
- **Button press-in (hover lift / active press, 80ms):** the only motion; it is tactile button feedback, an interaction state, not decoration (R-19). MOTION 2.
- **Error banner red shadow `#D61F1F`:** the shadow language encodes state — red shadow = error (functional, not decorative).
- **Dashed footer rule:** style-consistent separator for the footer credit; non-text, no contrast requirement.
- **Focus: 3px ink outline at 3px offset** outside the existing border: clearly distinguishable at 18:1 (R-32).
- **Logo stays 96px, no frame/border** (owner instruction from fix-001 follow-up: "murni aja").
