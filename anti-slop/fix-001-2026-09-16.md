## One-line reasons (R-31)

- **Background `#f3f5f8`:** flat official canvas so the white form reads as a document, not a scene.
- **Accent `#1d4f9c`:** one institutional blue, 7.9:1 with white, used only at focal moments (button, monogram, letterhead rule, focus ring).
- **Card shadow `0 1px 3px`:** the card is the only elevated surface on the page; the shadow separates it from the flat field (R-12 purpose).
- **3px letterhead rule:** the repeated identity motif of the screen, institutional letterhead pattern (R-20).
- **Monogram "SG":** honest placeholder, not disguised as a final logo (R-23), marked `// TODO`.
- **Typeface:** antd system-ui stack; institutional neutrality and maximum readability fit the dial-1 official language (R-06 reason).
- **Radii 10px card / 8px controls:** one consistent system, no pill shapes (R-11).
- **Motion:** hover background change only, matching MOTION 1 (R-19).
- **Footer `#5a6370`:** 6.0:1 on white, above WCAG AA (R-25).

## Contrast check (R-25)
