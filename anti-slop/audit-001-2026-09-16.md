# Anti Slop Audit 001 — Login Area

- **Date:** 2026-09-16
- **Mode:** 2 (post-completion audit)
- **Scope:** `src/components/Login.tsx`, login section of `src/app/globals.css` (lines 24-300 + responsive block ~469-510), pre-login loading state in `src/app/page.tsx`
- **Priority key:** Hard Gate = HIGH, Purpose-Gate = MEDIUM, Quality Locks = LOW

## HIGH — Hard Gate

### 1. R-37 — No design direction
No `DESIGN.md` or brand guidance exists in the project; the login was built without direction and is not labeled as a draft. A direction decision is required before any fix (owner: official/government style vs branded palette).

### 2. R-23 — Visual assets created without confirmation
Logo is an emoji (🏛️) inside a gradient circle; 📅 and 📊 emojis are used as icon content in the "Tahun Anggaran" select options. No explicit instructions, no honest placeholders.

### 3. R-25 — Text below WCAG AA contrast
`.footer-text` is `color: #999; opacity: 0.8` on the white card. Effective contrast ≈ 2.1:1; WCAG AA requires ≥ 4.5:1 for normal text.

### 4. R-35 — No verification record
The app has not been built/run and no click-through evidence of the login controls (form submit, error state, mobile layout) exists. Cannot claim PASS without running it; must be resolved during the fix.

## MEDIUM — Purpose-Gate

### 5. R-01 — Default blue→purple→pink gradient, no purpose
`.login-background` is `linear-gradient(135deg, #667eea 0%, #764ba2 50%, #f093fb 100%)` covering the full page. The same gradient also appears on the submit button, the title text, the logo circle, and the top shimmer bar. No hierarchy or brand purpose is written down. The pre-login loading screen in `src/app/page.tsx` reuses the same gradient.

### 6. R-07 — Dot/particle background without purpose
`.login-particles` is an animated field of white radial dots (20s loop) over the gradient; no visual-identity purpose documented.

### 7. R-13 — Glow applied to multiple elements simultaneously
Glow shadows appear on the submit button (`0 8px 24px rgba(102,126,234,0.3)`), the logo circle (`0 8px 24px rgba(102,126,234,0.3)`), and the three background orbs. Glow is not a focus accent on 1-2 important elements.

### 8. R-19 — Template animation stack without purpose
Simultaneous: particle drift (20s), orb float (8s), card fadeInUp (0.8s), top-bar shimmer (3s), logo pulse (2s), cursor blink, typing/delete loop, error shake. No UX purpose written down; no MOTION dial declared.

### 9. R-04 — Generic emoji icons
🏛️, 📅, 📊 serve as icon content with no documented relevance to the content they represent.

### 10. R-10 — Glassmorphism without purpose
`.login-card` uses `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)` on an rgba(255,255,255,0.95) card. The card is ~95% opaque, so the blur contributes nothing; it is decoration, not a hierarchy device.

### 11. R-06 — Typeface chosen without a written reason
Title and body use the antd default font stack plus gradient text treatment; no brand-character reason is documented for the typeface.

## LOW — Quality Locks

### 12. R-20 — Weak identity
Swap the logo and product name and the screen still reads as a generic gradient SaaS login. No palette, motif, or typographic voice specific to SI GEPENG.

### 13. R-31 — Decisions without written reasons
No one-line reason exists for the color, background, layout, or typography decisions (no `DESIGN.md`, no rationale in code).
